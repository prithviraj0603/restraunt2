THE PHILOSOPHY

A Modern Take on
Indian Haute Cuisine

Rooted in royal regional kitchens and guided by contemporary culinary alchemy, Ember & Spice honors ancestral cooking techniques while fearlessly redefining Indian fine dining.

From the smoldering embers of our open-flame sigri to delicate saffron and cardamom reductions from Kashmir, each plate is an evocative story of terrain, memory, and high sensory craft in the heart of Bangalore.

THE CULINARY DIRECTOR

Crafted with Intention,
Rooted in Fire.

“Our kitchen brings together traditional Indian culinary techniques passed down through royal generations, seasonal local ingredients from Karnataka, and contemporary presentation without diluting soul.”

— CHEF ARJUN MEHRA

Trained across iconic kitchens of Old Delhi, Lucknow, and Hyderabad, Arjun transitioned to vanguard dining spaces across Europe before returning to Bangalore with a singular obsession: to unearth rare heirloom spices and bring sacred open-flame cooking into Michelin-standard focus.
